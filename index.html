OUR STORY

About Maison De Fleur

Maison De Fleur began with a simple belief — that flowers should feel personal, not mass-produced. Every bouquet we send out is arranged by hand, to order, with the kind of care we'd want for our own family. No two are ever quite the same, and that's exactly the point.

GOOD TO KNOW

Delivery & Our Promise

Delivery within Hulhumalé — MVR 20. Hulhumalé to Malé MVR 30 · to speedboats MVR 30 · to the airport MVR 30.

Please order at least 3 days in advance so every bouquet gets the time it deserves.

Delivery is never charged when you confirm your order — only once your bouquet is ready and you'd like it delivered.

Your order is confirmed and started once payment is confirmed, and preparation usually begins right away.

If preparation hasn't started yet, a refund is possible. Once preparation has begun, we're unable to offer a refund.

If your bouquet arrives damaged on our side, we'll give you a full refund — or a partial refund based on the damage, if you'd still like to keep it.

Island deliveries are specially packed, and we send you a photo before it's secured onto the speedboat. Once it leaves our hands to the boat, we're unable to take responsibility or offer a refund for damage in transit.

Everything — price, size, timing — is told to you clearly before you confirm. No hidden fees, ever.
